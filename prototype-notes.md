# What already exists

Built the morning of 24 October, before the team brief was read. Treat it as parts, not as the product.

**Intent Desk** — https://claude.ai/artifact/75LhUr9cTWnRQNMKo6S45H (private; share it before sending the link
anywhere). A working queue: derived ICP, the searches it wrote, ranked people with the quote that qualified
them, a score, a why-now line, an opener drawn from their own post, a capture button that writes to a shared
store, and the filtered-out pile with reasons.

## What survives the brief

- The **page itself**, as the fallback board if Lightfern cannot hold a claimable lead list. The store is
  proven: a document was written, read back and deleted.
- **The filtered-out panel.** Showing what was dropped and why is the most persuasive thing on the screen.
- **The scoring shape**, intent and fit and urgency with a one line reason.
- **The Reddit findings.** A thread from this morning where recruiters tear apart a competitor's AI screening
  ad, including "automating the human part of recruitment is the dumbest idea" and "this is an ad written by
  AI". That is the evidence for keeping a human in the loop, and it belongs in the Loom.

## What does not survive

- **LinkedIn as a source.** Out of scope per the brief.
- **Email and phone enrichment.** Out of scope, and pointless against pseudonymous handles.
- **Retrivr as the demo company.** The demo is Jumpstart.
- **Hacker News is missing** from the prototype and is a Must in the brief.

## Reusable, concretely

The Reddit search through a logged-in browser works and needs no key, but the brief's free public endpoints are
simpler and will not rate limit on stage. Use the endpoints and keep the browser route as a backup.
